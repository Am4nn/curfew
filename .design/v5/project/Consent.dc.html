NEW TERMS

Before you carry on

Curfew has a coach in it now. He reads things, so this needs saying plainly before you carry on.
Ren reads everything you log Every check-in, every time, every number, and every photograph you have taken. That is new. Until now nothing read your pictures, and a model does now.
A photograph you share with a group is read for them too If you share Food with Wing, Wing's members see your meals, and their coaches read them the same way yours reads yours. Sharing a picture is sharing it with the coach behind the person.
He never decides whether a day counted Not one number in this app comes from a model. Passes, misses, streaks, standing and money are arithmetic over your check-ins and they stay that way.
Turn him off and off is real One switch in Settings. The tab goes, his lines go, and nothing of yours is sent to a model again. What is left is the tracker, which is a whole app on its own.
The money you owe is not deleted by anything on this screen, and never has been. A balance is a record of what two people agreed, so it survives you leaving.
