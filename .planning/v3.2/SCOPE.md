## Done

**16. The configure screen scrolled with the panel half empty.** Shipped in
`c2eb830`. The tab bar is `fixed bottom-0` over every route and every screen
reserves `pb-nav`, `calc(6rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`. This one reserved
`pb-16`, 4rem, so 32px plus the home indicator sat under the bar. On the setup
flow that is the Next button. Also dropped `overflow-y-auto` from the panel: it
made a scroller inside a page that already scrolls and could never clip anything,
since its parent is `min-h-dvh`, a minimum, so `flex-1` had no upper bound.
**Not confirmed on a device.** CI cannot see it either: nothing here measures
where a fixed bar lands.

**21. Users being logged out.** Not an expiry problem. Sessions are 90 days with
a one-day refresh (`src/lib/auth.ts`). The likely cause is iOS evicting storage
for a home-screen PWA after about a week without opening it, and no server
setting changes that. Closed unless it recurs with a device and a duration.

**24. Deleting a group.** Not being built. Groups are not deletable and members
leave if they want to. A group that reaches zero members is archived by the
nightly job. One edge case to handle when that lands: an owner leaving is how a
group reaches zero, so leaving has to either refuse the last owner or hand over
first.

**25. Haptics.** `navigator.vibrate` behind feature detection. Android Chrome
gets it, iOS gets nothing: WebKit exposes no vibration API at all. The known
trick, a hidden `<input type="checkbox" switch>` whose toggle fires the system
haptic engine, is an exploit Apple is reported to have closed in iOS 26.5, and
injecting a hidden checkbox on every press is the kind of thing that breaks
silently. Not shipping it.

## The engine

**17. Sleep: new windows, and a confirm anchored to an event.**

- Night 9:30 PM to 11:00 PM. Wake 5:30 AM to 7:00 AM.
- **The confirm window opens 30 minutes after the Wake press and stays open 30
  minutes.** `confirm_open` and `confirm_close` leave the config schema.
- This is the redesign, not the strings: every window in the app until now has
  been a clock time in config, and this one is anchored to another press.
  `check:offer` exists to prove a control is offered exactly when a press would
  count, so it moves with this.
- The confirm survives because it carries the only photograph sleep asks for
  (decision 45). Night and wake are bare timestamps. A confirm you place
  yourself can be placed at an hour you are already up.
- **No ambiguity about which Wake press anchors it.** Sleep's steps do not set
  `repeats`, so each is one-time: the button is spent once recorded
  (`checkin.ts:225`) and a second press returns "already recorded"
  (`checkin.ts:543`). There can only be one.
- Existing users get the new config written with `effective_from` tomorrow,
  which invariant 4 requires anyway, and hear about it through the release note.

**18. `minGap` defaults.** Water 30 minutes, meals 90. Currently 0, which is why
eight glasses in eight seconds passes the day. The schema and the rule text
already exist (`schedule.ts:58`, `rule.ts:93`); only the default is wrong.
Overridable per user.

**19 and 20. Grace becomes one pool, spent by hand.**

- **Two a month for each activity tracked, counted live.** Add a sixth activity
  today and the pool is 12 today.
- **Fungible.** Spend it all on one activity if you like. That is allowed on
  purpose: grace holds a streak and nothing else, so doing it costs a fine and a
  dip in standing every time and needs no rule to discourage. A per-activity cap
  would collapse the pool back into today's allowance with a button on it.
- **One grace per missed DAY.** A gym week wanting three sessions that got one is
  two days short and costs two.
- **Manual.** The streak ends, and grace is an offer to undo that. Recorded as an
  event, so `verify` can replay it and invariant 1 holds.
- **The offer opens when the streak breaks and closes when you next check in for
  that activity.** Floor one day, ceiling two weeks.
- **Not affordable means no offer on Home at all.** A disabled control is a thing
  to wonder about on the screen looked at most. The grace screen keeps the offer,
  drops its button and says what it needs against what you have, which makes it
  the one place that explains the row.
- `schedule.grace` stops being a per-activity setting. Decide at build time
  whether the column goes or is left ignored.
- Grace still protects the streak ONLY. The fine posts and reputation dips either
  way, which is decision 5 and was true before any of this.

## Sharing and surfaces

**15. A photograph belongs to the groups it was sent to.**

- **Tagged at the check-in that sends it**, to the groups sharing that activity
  at that moment, and never again. A group added to your sharing tomorrow gets
  tomorrow's photographs and never this one.
- **Visibility starts at the join INSTANT**, not the start of that day.
  `joinedFrom` currently rounds down to `startOf("day")`.
- **Shared during grace.** Grace means the group is not scoring you yet, not that
  you have not consented.
- **Revoked, not deleted, on unshare or leave.** A `revoked_at` on the tag keeps
  an audit of what was visible when, matching the append-only style of
  `ledger_entries`, and rejoining or re-sharing never brings a photograph back.
- **No backfill.** There are no photographs to carry across.
- **Check-in says nothing about groups.** The tagging happens at that press but
  it is not a decision made there, and a list of group names on the camera
  screen would read as one. Where a photograph went is answered on Your Photos,
  where a struck-through group saw it once and cannot any more.
- Today `groupEvidence` reads `sharesFor`, the CURRENT share state, which is the
  leak: turn sharing on and last week's photographs appear.

**22. Stopping an activity states what it costs.** A bare `<form action>` today.
Four consequences, one permanent: the streak goes to 0, the activity un-shares
from every group, the photographs those groups have seen leave for good, and the
ceiling drops. `ConfirmButton` already exists.

**23. Home keeps Create a group** until you are actually in one. It is currently
behind `inAGroup` AND an empty day, so adding one activity removes the only route
to groups from Home.

**26. Archive leaves the admin groups LIST.** It is on the row
(`group-row.tsx:54`) and again on the detail page.

**27. `.design/` is published.** Currently gitignored, and thirteen artboards
already existed only on the canvas because nothing tracked them. Check the
landing images for the Gemini provenance watermark before making it public.

## Release notes

Written in `release-notes.json` at the repo root, in the same commit as the
change. `bun run publish:notice -- --version 3.2.0 --as <email> --dry` first.

**The bar is not "this changed".** The overlay blocks the whole app, so an entry
that did not need reading teaches people to dismiss it unread. The test is
whether somebody has to DO something differently tomorrow. 3.2.0 announces the
sleep window and grace, and does not announce the photograph fix: real, an
improvement, and nobody has to act on it.
